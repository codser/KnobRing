KnobRing

Wheel, scrubbing and transforms for Premiere Pro
App

Click a slot to edit it. Up to 12 slots. Changes save straight away.

Slot

Type it, or press Record and then the keys.

Opens Position X, Position Y, Scale, Rotation and Opacity for the selected clips. Pick one, press the knob, then turn. Step sizes are on the Transforms tab.

Scrub the timeline

Knob scrubs in Premiere
While Premiere is in front and the wheel is closed, turning the knob moves the playhead instead of the volume.
Mouse wheel scrubs
Scrolling anywhere over Premiere moves the playhead. Turn off to scroll panels normally.

Frames per click

Slow · hold Ctrl
Normal
Fast · hold Shift

One knob click or one wheel notch moves this many frames. Tip: set Fast to your frame rate to jump a second at a time.

Step per knob click

NormalFine · CtrlBig · Shift

Position in pixels, Scale and Opacity in %, Rotation in degrees. Applies in After Effects too.

How to use

Select clips on the timeline, press the knob, choose Transforms, pick a property and press. Turn to change it, press again to pick another. Keyframed properties get a keyframe at the playhead.
